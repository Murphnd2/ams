Resources for Employees and Families

Learn about your benefit accounts and how to make the most of them.

Flexible Spending Accounts

Set aside money from each paycheck, before taxes, to help pay for IRS-approved out-of-pocket medical expenses for you and your dependents.

Learn More

Health Savings Accounts

A tax-advantaged account for people enrolled in a high-deductible health plan. Pre-tax contributions cover eligible expenses, and unused funds roll over year to year.

Learn More

Health Reimbursement Arrangements

An employer-funded benefit plan that reimburses you tax-free for qualified healthcare expenses. Your employer determines which expenses qualify.

Learn More

COBRA Benefits

Keep healthcare coverage under your employer's group plan after a qualifying event such as job loss, reduced hours, divorce, or other life changes.

Our Services

Professional benefits administration with a personal touch.

FSA Administration

Healthcare FSAs, Dependent Care FSAs, Limited Purpose FSAs, and Premium Only Plans (POP). Increase payroll tax savings, boost employee satisfaction, and enjoy flexible plan options.

HSA Administration

One-stop HSA management — account administration, custodial banking, and integrated investments all under one roof. Paired with a qualified HDHP for triple tax advantages.

HRA Administration

Group health HRAs, ICHRAs, EBHRAs, and QSEHRAs. Employer-funded, fully customizable reimbursement accounts with Claims Crossover for the fastest possible claims payment.

COBRA Administration

Every detail handled correctly, every time. Timely notifications, election tracking, flexible payment options including ACH and mobile app, and full regulatory compliance.

Transit & Commuter Accounts

Tax-free employee contributions for parking and mass transit expenses. Our stacked benefits debit card puts multiple accounts on one card for maximum convenience.

Lifestyle Spending Accounts

Employer-funded wellness benefits that employees love — fitness, financial wellness, personal development. Simple to offer, easy to administer, great for retention.
