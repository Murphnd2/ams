How to Access Your Benefits Portal

A Quick Guide for Plan Participants

1

Open the Portal

Open your web browser and navigate to your employer's benefits portal URL. This link is typically provided in your enrollment welcome email.

2

Enter Your Email

On the login page, enter the email address associated with your benefits account. This is usually your work email provided during enrollment.

3

Enter Your Password

Enter the password you created during initial account setup. If this is your first time logging in, use the temporary password from your welcome email and follow the prompts to create a new one.

4

Access Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see your benefits dashboard. From here you can view account balances, submit claims, check transaction history, and manage your account settings.

Need Help?

If you are unable to log in or have forgotten your password, contact your benefits administrator or call the support number provided in your enrollment materials.
