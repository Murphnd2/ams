What Can an LSA Cover?

The employer decides which categories qualify. These are some of the most popular options — but every plan is unique. Superior State can help you design the right mix for your workforce.

Physical Wellness

  • Gym, health club, and fitness studio memberships
  • Exercise and athletic equipment
  • Personal trainers and fitness classes
  • Park passes, race entry fees, and recreational sports leagues

Financial & Professional Wellness

  • Financial advisor and planning services
  • Remote work setup (internet, furniture, equipment)
  • Personal development classes (art, cooking, music, professional skills)

Personal & Family Wellness

  • Meditation classes and wellness apps
  • Pet care (walkers, daycare, grooming)
  • Travel and camping (airfare, lodging, equipment, park fees)

Why Small Employers Love LSAs

Employees Notice It

LSAs cover things employees actually spend money on — gym memberships, fitness gear, pet care, personal development. It feels personal in a way that a health plan deductible never will.

It's Affordable

At $500–$1,000 per employee, an LSA costs a fraction of a health plan enhancement. With a use-it-or-lose-it design, unused funds come back to the employer.

It's Flexible

The employer controls the categories, the budget, and the plan design. There's no one-size-fits-all — you build it around what your employees value.

It's Simple

No IRS limits to track. No nondiscrimination testing. No plan document updates every time the IRS publishes new numbers. Minimal administrative overhead.

It Helps You Compete

Large employers are adding LSAs at a rapid pace. Offering one signals that your company invests in employee wellbeing — without the cost structure of a large-employer benefits program.
