Why Not Handle It In-House?

Many small employers try to manage COBRA internally — until they realize the cost of a mistake. COBRA compliance requires:

  • Tracking multiple qualifying events with overlapping deadlines
  • Preparing legally compliant notices
  • Collecting premiums from former employees
  • Maintaining documentation that can withstand an audit

For a small HR team already stretched thin, COBRA is a prime candidate for outsourcing.
