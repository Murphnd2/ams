HRA Decision Guide

Use the scenarios below to identify which plan type fits your situation. Not sure? Superior State can walk you through the options and help you design the plan that fits your workforce and budget.

"We offer a group health plan and want to help employees with out-of-pocket costs."

→ Traditional HRA (consumer-driven, with carryover) or MERP (cost-savings-driven, no carryover)

"We want to reduce our group plan premiums without cutting employee benefits."

→ MERP — purchase a high-deductible plan and fund the gap. Premium savings of 40–50% are common.

"We don't want to manage a group plan. We'd rather fund employees' individual coverage."

→ ICHRA (any employer size) or QSEHRA (fewer than 50 employees)

"We already offer a group plan and want to add dental/vision dollars for all employees."

→ EBHRA — supplements the group plan; employees don't have to enroll to participate.

"We're considering self-insurance but the complexity concerns us."

→ MERP — achieves similar risk-versus-reward with lower admin costs and a simpler structure.
