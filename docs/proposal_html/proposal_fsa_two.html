Four Types of FSAs — Each Designed for a Different Need

Superior State administers all four FSA types, giving you the flexibility to offer the right benefit for your workforce — whether your employees need help with medical costs, childcare, dental and vision, or individual insurance premiums.

💬
Healthcare FSA
The most widely used FSA type. Employees set aside pre-tax dollars for qualified medical, dental, and vision expenses not covered by insurance — copays, deductibles, prescriptions, eyeglasses, orthodontia, physical therapy, mental health services, and hundreds of OTC products.
  • 2026 limit: $3,400 per employee
  • Full annual election available on day one
  • No health plan enrollment required
  • Employer may also contribute to accounts
👪
Dependent Care FSA (DCAP)
Helps working parents and caregivers pay for daycare, preschool, before/after school programs, summer day camps, and elder care expenses.
  • 2026 limit: $7,500 ($3,750 if married filing separately)
  • NEW FOR 2026: 50% increase under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act
  • Funds available as contributed, not upfront
  • Eligible: children under 13, or dependents incapable of self-care
👁
Limited Purpose FSA (LPFSA)
A specialized FSA for employees who have a Health Savings Account (HSA). Covers dental and vision expenses only, preserving HSA eligibility — essential for any employer offering an HDHP.
  • 2026 limit: $3,400 per employee
  • Carryover up to $680 if plan allows
  • Eligible: eye exams, glasses, contacts, dental, LASIK, braces
💙
Individual Premium Reimbursement FSA
Allows employees to reimburse individually purchased health-related insurance premiums — dental, vision, critical illness, cancer, hospital indemnity, accident, disability. Not major medical.
  • Separate election — not subject to the $3,400 limit
  • Employees can participate in both simultaneously
  • Strictly use-it-or-lose-it — no carryover or grace period
  • Ideal when employer doesn’t offer group supplemental coverage

Why it matters for small employers: Many small employers don’t offer group dental, vision, or supplemental coverage. Employees often purchase these policies individually with after-tax dollars. The Individual Premium Reimbursement FSA gives them a way to pay those premiums pre-tax — saving hundreds per year at no cost to you.
