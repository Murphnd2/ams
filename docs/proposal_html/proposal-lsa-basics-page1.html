Lifestyle Spending Accounts (LSA)

A Modern Benefit Your
Employees Will Actually Use

LSAs are one of the fastest-growing employee benefits in the market — and one of the simplest to offer. An LSA is an employer-funded account that reimburses employees for wellness and lifestyle expenses that traditional benefit plans don't cover. For small employers competing for talent against companies with deeper pockets, an LSA is a smart, affordable way to stand out.

The Basics

Employer-Funded

The employer sets a per-employee budget — typically $500 to $1,000 per year — with no required minimum or maximum.

Employer-Designed

The employer chooses which expense categories are eligible, tailored to company culture and workforce priorities.

Reimbursement-Based

Employees pay for eligible expenses out of pocket, then submit a receipt for reimbursement. That's it.

Taxable Income

Reimbursements are included in the employee's taxable income (reported on the W-2) — the tradeoff for zero compliance burden.

Use-It-or-Lose-It

Employers can design the LSA so unused funds do not carry over, helping control costs year to year.

Unlike FSAs, HRAs, or HSAs — an LSA is not a tax-advantaged account. That means no IRS contribution limits, no complex compliance rules, and no plan document headaches. Simple by design.
