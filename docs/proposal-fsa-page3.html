Flexible Spending Accounts

The Benefit That Pays for Itself

Most employees are already spending money on healthcare — copays, prescriptions, glasses, dental work, daycare. A Flexible Spending Account lets them pay for those expenses with pre-tax dollars, which means more take-home pay on every paycheck. And because pre-tax contributions reduce your payroll tax obligation as the employer, you save money too.

How the Savings Stack Up

For every dollar an employee contributes to an FSA, they save roughly 20–30% in combined federal income tax, state income tax, and FICA/Medicare taxes.

On the employer side, you save 7.65% in FICA/Medicare matching on every pre-tax dollar. For a company with 20 employees averaging $2,000 each in FSA contributions, that’s over $3,000 a year in employer payroll tax savings — with zero additional cost to offer the benefit.
