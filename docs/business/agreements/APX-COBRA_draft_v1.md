# COBRA AND CONTINUATION SERVICES APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-COBRA**

Employer has independently concluded that one or more of its benefit plans providing health care is subject to the continuation coverage requirements of the **Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1985, as amended ("COBRA")**, and has asked SSA to assist it in satisfying its obligations as plan administrator, as set forth in this Appendix.

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement"). Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date assigned by SSA as stated in the SSA client welcome letter. The responsibilities set forth here are in addition to those set forth in the Agreement.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the continuation coverage services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law. The Business Associate Agreement controls as to the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information, subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability, which applies to it.

**Applicability is Employer's determination.** Employer determines whether COBRA applies to it and to each of its plans, including the twenty-employee threshold and the aggregation of related entities under a controlled group, and determines which plans are Covered Benefit Plans for purposes of this Appendix. SSA relies on that determination and makes none of its own.

---

## A. Services SSA provides

SSA will provide the following, for Covered Benefit Plans identified by Employer:

**A.1 General (initial) notice.** By first class mail with proof of mailing, to covered employees and, where addressed jointly is not appropriate, to covered spouses, for individuals who first become covered under a Covered Benefit Plan after the effective date of this Appendix — **and, if elected in Article VII of the Agreement, to all employees and spouses covered under a Covered Benefit Plan on the effective date of this Appendix.**

**A.2 Qualifying event notice (election notice).** By first class mail with proof of mailing and certification by the U.S. Postal Service, to qualified beneficiaries for qualifying events occurring after the effective date of this Appendix.

**A.3 Notice of unavailability of continuation coverage.** To an individual who provides notice of a qualifying event, second qualifying event, or disability determination and who SSA determines, applying Employer's written plan terms, is not entitled to continuation coverage, as required by 29 C.F.R. §2590.606-4(c).

**A.4 Notice of early termination of continuation coverage.** To a qualified beneficiary whose continuation coverage terminates before the end of the maximum coverage period, as required by 29 C.F.R. §2590.606-4(d).

**A.5 Update notices.** By first class mail to covered employees, COBRA participants, and qualified beneficiaries as required by amendments to COBRA or its regulations, or by changes to Covered Benefit Plans.

**A.6 Open enrollment and rate change notices.** To COBRA participants and qualified beneficiaries in an election period, based on plan options and rates Employer furnishes under Section B.5.

**A.7 Intake of beneficiary-initiated notices.** Receipt and processing of notices from covered employees and qualified beneficiaries of divorce or legal separation, a dependent ceasing to be a dependent, a second qualifying event, and a disability determination, and application of Employer's written plan procedures to them.

**A.8 Election, extension, and termination processing.** Processing of elections, waivers and revocations of waivers, disability extensions, second qualifying events, Medicare entitlement, and terminations, in accordance with Employer's written plan terms and applicable law.

**A.9 Billing, premium collection, and remittance** as provided in Section D.

**A.10 Notice to Employer** on non-payment of premium, on the end of a COBRA term, and of any discrepancy or condition SSA discovers that may require action by Employer.

**A.11 Records and reporting.** SSA will maintain records of all notices, and a database of qualified beneficiaries to whom notifications have been sent including the information reported by Employer; provide Employer with confirmation reports of activity; and notify Employer of known changes or updates relating to compliance with COBRA.

## B. Employer responsibilities

**B.1 Implementation data.** Before services commence, Employer will submit: a complete roster of all employees who are active participants in Covered Benefit Plans, in electronic or spreadsheet format; a completed and signed questionnaire (or online survey) identifying the qualified health plans it offers; and a completed data sheet for each of the following — existing COBRA participants, qualified beneficiaries who have been notified and are within an election period, and qualified beneficiaries who have not been notified.

**B.2 Ongoing notice to SSA.** Following commencement, Employer will notify SSA through SSA's benefits website or by secure file transfer, **within fourteen (14) days** of the date it has knowledge of: employees who have become eligible for and elected a Covered Benefit Plan; and any qualifying event, including death of a covered employee, termination of employment, reduction of hours causing loss of coverage, divorce or legal separation, a covered employee's entitlement to Medicare, a dependent ceasing to be a dependent, and changes to Covered Benefit Plans including insurers, rates, or benefits.

**B.3 SSA's deadlines run from receipt of data.** Consistent with the Agreement, **SSA's notice obligations under Section A run from SSA's receipt of complete and accurate data from Employer, not from the date of the underlying event.** Late, incomplete, or inaccurate data extends SSA's deadlines accordingly, and any resulting excise tax, penalty, statutory damages, or liability is Employer's. Employer acknowledges that COBRA's statutory framework allocates a limited period between the event and the required notice, that Employer's fourteen-day obligation in Section B.2 exists to preserve SSA's ability to perform within it, and that data furnished late may make timely notice impossible.

**B.4 Carrier and plan administration.** **Employer manages all coverage change notifications to the applicable carriers, including enrollment, reinstatement, and termination**, unless otherwise agreed in writing for an agreed increase in fees. SSA does not enroll, reinstate, or terminate coverage with any carrier.

**B.5 Rates and plan options.** Employer will furnish applicable premium rates, plan options, and any rate change **at least `[thirty (30)]` days before the effective date of the change**, together with the administrative fee Employer directs SSA to apply under Section D.5. SSA bills the rates Employer furnishes and makes no rate determination.

**B.6 Funding of claims and premiums.** Employer is responsible for payment of premiums to carriers and for benefits payable under Covered Benefit Plans. SSA does not pay carriers and does not advance premium.

**B.7 Review.** Employer will review SSA's confirmation and activity reports and notify SSA of any error of which it is aware within `[thirty (30)]` days.

## C. Representations and warranties

Employer warrants and represents that it is the legal plan administrator and sole fiduciary of each Covered Benefit Plan, and will not require any bond or security of SSA in the performance of its duties under this Appendix. Employer reserves full authority to make all decisions regarding its COBRA administration, including those delegated to SSA by this Appendix. **SSA serves as a service provider under the direction of the plan administrator and does not act as the plan administrator.** Employer will take all necessary action and execute all necessary documents to authorize SSA to perform its functions under this Appendix, and will timely provide the information and documentation SSA reasonably requests.

## D. Premiums — collection, custody, and remittance

**This Section describes the one circumstance in which SSA receives funds. Read it against Section 1.6 of the Agreement.**

**D.1 Collection as agent.** SSA collects continuation coverage premiums from qualified beneficiaries **solely as agent of Employer**. Amounts so collected are held for remittance to Employer as provided in this Section. **SSA does not hold such amounts in trust, is not a trustee or fiduciary with respect to them, and acquires no beneficial interest in them.** ⚠️ `[Decision: account titling and segregation — see notes.]`

**D.2 Remittance.** SSA will remit collected premiums to Employer `[monthly, on or before the ___ day of the month following collection]`, together with a remittance report identifying the qualified beneficiaries, coverage months, and amounts to which the remittance relates. **Employer is responsible for remitting premium to its carriers.**

**D.3 Interest.** `[Decision: Any interest or earnings on collected premiums are retained by SSA as part of its compensation under this Appendix / are remitted to Employer.]`

**D.4 Payment periods and shortfalls.** SSA will apply the payment periods and rules of Employer's written plan terms and applicable law, including the forty-five (45) day period for an initial payment, the thirty (30) day grace period for subsequent payments, and the treatment of an insignificant shortfall. **SSA will not accept or apply a payment that Employer's plan terms do not permit it to accept.**

**D.5 Administrative fee.** Where Employer directs, SSA will add an administrative fee to the premium charged, not exceeding the maximum permitted by law — generally two percent (2%) of the applicable premium, or such other amount as law permits during a disability extension. **SSA retains the administrative fee it collects as part of its compensation under this Appendix.**

**D.6 Self-insured and account-based plans.** Where a Covered Benefit Plan is self-insured or is an account-based plan, including a health reimbursement arrangement, **Employer determines the applicable premium on an actuarially reasonable basis and certifies it to SSA.** SSA bills the amount Employer certifies, makes no actuarial determination, and — because there is no insurer premium to load — SSA's compensation for such plans is as stated in the Fee Schedule rather than under Section D.5 unless Employer directs otherwise.

**D.7 Returned and reversed payments.** A returned, dishonored, or reversed beneficiary payment is treated as non-payment for the period to which it relates, subject to the plan's payment periods. **SSA does not advance premium and does not guarantee collection.** A fee of `[$25.00]` may be assessed for each returned payment where the plan permits.

**D.8 Risk of lapse.** **Employer bears the risk of any loss of, or failure to reinstate, coverage arising from Employer's failure to effect timely enrollment, reinstatement, or termination with a carrier, or from Employer's failure to remit premium to a carrier.** SSA is not liable for a lapse of coverage except to the extent it results directly and proximately from SSA's breach of the Standard of Care.

**D.9 Overpayments and refunds.** SSA will apply or refund an overpayment in accordance with Employer's written plan terms. Where a refund is due after SSA has remitted the related premium to Employer, Employer will fund the refund.

## E. Continuation of an account-based plan (HRA, ICHRA, EBHRA, MERP)

**E.1 The arrangement itself is the continuable coverage.** Where a Covered Benefit Plan is a health reimbursement arrangement, including an individual coverage HRA, **the arrangement is the coverage subject to continuation. Any individual health insurance coverage that the arrangement reimburses is not group health plan coverage and is not continued by, or guaranteed by, continuation of the arrangement.**

**E.2 Premium.** Determined and certified by Employer under Section D.6.

**E.3 Substantiation.** A qualified beneficiary continuing an individual coverage HRA remains subject to the same substantiation requirements as an active participant, including annual and per-request substantiation of enrollment in individual health insurance coverage. **Failure to substantiate holds reimbursement for the affected period; it does not by itself terminate continuation coverage.** Where the arrangement's terms require that the individual coverage be purchased outside an Exchange, that requirement continues to apply.

**E.4 No card for a non-employee.** ⚠️ **A qualified beneficiary who is not an active employee will not be issued or permitted to retain an electronic payment card**, consistent with the card program provider's requirements. Reimbursement to such a qualified beneficiary is made by the methods described in the applicable appendix.

**E.5 Special enrollment.** Loss of eligibility for an individual coverage HRA may give rise to a special enrollment period in the individual market. SSA's notices state the fact of the loss and its effective date. **SSA does not advise a qualified beneficiary regarding individual coverage, does not recommend or select any plan, and does not enroll any individual in any coverage.** ⚠️ Timing and mechanics of any such special enrollment period are outside SSA's services; Employer and its broker or agent of record address them.

## F. Scope limitations

**F.1 State continuation.** Continuation coverage required by state law ("mini-COBRA") and continuation required of governmental employers under the Public Health Service Act are **outside the scope of this Appendix** unless expressly elected by addendum. Employer determines whether any such requirement applies to it.

**F.2 Regulatory relief and extraordinary deadline changes.** Where a governmental action extends, tolls, or otherwise modifies a COBRA election, notice, or payment deadline, SSA will implement the change and **may revise fees under the Agreement** to reflect the resulting change in scope. Employer bears the cost of extended administration.

**F.3 Other services excluded.** SSA provides no service under this Appendix other than as described in Section A, including no carrier enrollment, no rate negotiation, no plan design, and no assistance with reporting or disclosure obligations allocated to Employer under the Agreement.

---

## G. Termination

On termination of this Appendix or the Agreement: SSA ceases sending notices and collecting premium as of the effective date of termination; SSA remits to Employer all premiums collected and not yet remitted, less amounts due to SSA; SSA delivers records as provided in the Agreement; and notices and payments received after the effective date of termination are forwarded to Employer or, at Employer's request, to another administrator. Employer remains responsible for its obligations as plan administrator throughout.
