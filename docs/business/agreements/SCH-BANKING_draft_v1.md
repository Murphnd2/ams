## Part 3 — Authorization and warranties

By signing below, Employer:

1. **Authorizes SSA** to originate ACH debit entries against the account designated in Part 2, and credit entries and adjustments necessary to correct an erroneous entry, in amounts that vary with benefit payment and card activity, for the services elected in Part 1. Where card settlement is elected, Employer authorizes the card program provider and its issuing bank to originate settlement entries against that account.

2. **Warrants** that the information in Part 2 is complete and accurate, that the account is owned by Employer, and that the individual signing is authorized to grant this authorization and to authorize debits against the account. **Employer will notify SSA in writing of any change before it takes effect**, and is responsible for losses resulting from inaccurate or outdated information it has furnished.

3. **Agrees to maintain sufficient available funds** in the account to cover all entries originated and all checks released under the elected services.

4. **Acknowledges the reimbursement obligation.** If a funding or settlement debit is returned or not finally collected, Employer will reimburse SSA on demand for any participant payment already settled, together with the returned-entry fee and interest, as provided in APX-PAYMENT Section D. **Employer acknowledges that a participant payment already settled generally cannot be recovered by reversal.**

5. **Acknowledges suspension.** SSA may suspend payment services and card funding, without terminating the Agreement, upon a returned entry or a failure to reimburse, and repeated returned entries may result in permanent elimination of the direct deposit option for Employer's participants.

6. **Understands this authorization remains in effect** until Employer revokes it by written notice to SSA in such time and manner as to afford SSA and the financial institution a reasonable opportunity to act on it. Revocation does not affect entries already originated or checks already released, and does not relieve Employer of the obligations in paragraph 4.

7. **Acknowledges** that SSA is **not a signatory on and has no independent access to** the account, holds no title to funds in it, and takes no custody of Employer or plan funds.

## Part 5 — Card settlement acknowledgments *(complete only if 1D elected)*

Cards are issued by a bank under a program agreement between the issuing bank and SSA's card program provider. SSA is not the issuer, is not a bank, and extends no credit. Employer acknowledges and agrees that:

1. **Employer is responsible to the card program provider** for settlement of card transactions against the account designated in Part 2.
2. **A charge of $25.00 is assessed to Employer for each ACH entry returned due to insufficient funds.**
3. **The card program provider reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to terminate access to its system by Employer's plan participants** if payment obligations are not paid in full in a timely manner.
4. The card program provider is an **intended third-party beneficiary** of paragraphs 1 through 3 of this Part 5 and of Part 3, and this Schedule may be produced to it on request.
5. A missed or unfunded payroll results in no card funding for the affected period and **never in participant debt.**
6. Card disputes, chargebacks, and lost or stolen cards are handled under the issuer's processes and the cardholder agreement delivered with the card, to which SSA is not a party.

## Notes to Kevin

1. **This discharges both halves of the DataPath acknowledgment.** Part 5 carries the three warranted flow-downs, Part 3 carries the ACH authorization, and the executed page is retained and producible. ⚠️ Still contingent on the question from two turns ago: whether DataPath accepts an SSA-provided form, or requires a current version of its own. Their popup says "the settlement form provided by Customer," which reads like yours.
2. **One account or several.** Drafted as a single designated account serving all elected services, which is the simplest thing to administer and to authorize. If any employer wants card settlement segregated from benefit payments, Part 2 needs to repeat per service — worth deciding before the AdobeSign template is built rather than after.
3. **⚠️ Part 5 paragraph 2 names $25 and APX-PAYMENT names $50.** That is intentional — $25 is the card provider's fee on a card settlement return, $50 is yours on a payment-rail return. Different rails, different fees. If that reads as inconsistent to a client, set them equal in your own documents and let the provider's $25 stand where it applies.
4. **The third-party beneficiary designation in Part 5.4 is why the MSA's no-third-party-beneficiary clause needs a carve-out** rather than being written flat.
5. **AdobeSign field notes.** Part 1 checkboxes drive conditional visibility of Parts 4 and 5. Routing, account, and EIN fields should be marked as restricted data; the signature specimen is an attachment, not a form field. `Proposal #` should carry the AMS proposal id.
6. **The uploaded Hemlock form is a live document with real account and routing numbers and an EIN.** Nothing from it is reproduced here, and nothing from it should go into a template or a test record.
