# QUALIFIED SMALL EMPLOYER HRA RIDER
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Rider reference: **RIDER-QSEHRA**

Employer has established or will establish a **qualified small employer health reimbursement arrangement** ("QSEHRA") under Internal Revenue Code §9831(d), and has elected QSEHRA administration services in Article VII of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement").

This Rider is incorporated into and made a part of the Agreement and supplements the **Reimbursement Account Administration Appendix (APX-RA)**. Sections A through F of APX-RA apply to the QSEHRA except as this Rider provides otherwise.

**Order of Precedence.** As to any QSEHRA-specific matter, this Rider controls over APX-RA. As to the scope and performance of reimbursement account services generally, APX-RA controls. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law. The Business Associate Agreement controls as to the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information, subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability, which applies to it.

**A QSEHRA is a distinct arrangement.** ⚠️ Employer acknowledges that a QSEHRA is governed by a different statutory regime than an individual coverage HRA, that the two may not be combined, and that **no provision of RIDER-ICHRA applies to a QSEHRA.**

---

## A. Services SSA provides

In addition to the services described in APX-RA, SSA will:

**A.1 Documents.** Prepare the text of initial drafts of the QSEHRA plan document and summary plan description, furnished for Employer's review with its own legal counsel; adoption and execution are Employer's alone.

**A.2 Employee notice.** Prepare the written notice required to be furnished to each eligible employee under §9831(d)(4), containing the elements that provision requires — including the amount of the employee's permitted benefit, the requirement that the employee report the permitted benefit when applying for advance payment of the premium tax credit, and the tax consequences of failing to maintain minimum essential coverage — and deliver it through SSA's platform or by mail as Employer directs, **on the schedule Employer establishes under Section C.**

**A.3 Permitted benefit calculation.** Calculate each eligible employee's permitted benefit for the year based on the contribution structure, eligibility data, and applicable annual limit **Employer specifies**, and make the calculation available to Employer and reflect it in the notice under A.2.

**A.4 Substantiation.** Administer the substantiation program described in Section D.

**A.5 Reimbursement.** Process reimbursement of, or direct payment of, substantiated individual health insurance premiums and other eligible medical expenses to the extent the QSEHRA's terms provide, by the funding methods elected under APX-RA Section C, including the electronic payment card under APX-CARD.

**A.6 W-2 data.** At Employer's written request, furnish the permitted benefit amount for each employee for the year, for Employer's use in its Form W-2 reporting.

**A.7 Records.** Maintain records of eligibility, permitted benefit calculation, notice delivery, substantiation, and reimbursement activity.

**A.8 Renewal.** Administer annual renewal, including re-solicitation of substantiation and recalculation of permitted benefits on the data Employer specifies for the new year.

---

## B. Determinations reserved to Employer

Employer makes each of the following determinations. **SSA implements what Employer determines, relies on it under Section 5.2 of the Agreement, and makes no determination of its own:**

**B.1 Eligible employer status.** That Employer is an eligible employer for QSEHRA purposes — that it is **not an applicable large employer** and **does not offer a group health plan to any employee**, including any health flexible spending arrangement or other group health plan, for the same period.

**B.2 Eligible employees and same terms.** Which employees are eligible, the application of any permitted exclusion, and that the QSEHRA is provided **on the same terms to all eligible employees**, subject to the permitted variation based on the price of an insurance policy in the relevant individual market by reference to age and family size.

**B.3 Annual limit.** The applicable statutory annual limit for the year, as indexed, and that each permitted benefit is within it. ⚠️ **SSA does not determine the statutory limit or its indexed amount for any year; Employer specifies it.**

**B.4 Contribution structure.** The permitted benefit amounts and any permitted variation.

**B.5 Reporting.** Form W-2 reporting of the permitted benefit, Form 720 (PCORI) and Form 5500 to the extent Employer determines they apply, and the obligations allocated in Section 2.3 of the Agreement and APX-RA Section B.9.

**B.6 ERISA and plan document obligations.** ⚠️ The ERISA status of the QSEHRA and of the individual health insurance coverage it reimburses, and any resulting summary plan description, disclosure, or reporting obligation.

**B.7 Employee interaction with the Marketplace.** That the notice under A.2 is furnished, and that Employer does not direct, endorse, or steer any employee toward or away from any particular coverage or any particular purchase channel.

**B.8 Funding.** Funding of the QSEHRA and reimbursement of amounts settled in advance of collection, as provided in APX-PAYMENT and APX-CARD.

---

## C. Notice timing

⚠️ **Employer establishes the QSEHRA's year, eligibility dates, and the schedule on which the required notice is furnished. SSA prepares and delivers the notice on that schedule and does not determine, compute, or represent the date by which the notice is required to be furnished.** No date is stated in this Rider. Employer is responsible for determining the applicable deadline — including for a newly established QSEHRA, for an employee who becomes eligible during the year, and for a year beginning on a date other than January 1 — in consultation with its own counsel.

Employer will furnish SSA the data necessary to prepare and deliver each notice sufficiently in advance for SSA to perform. **Section 5.3 of the Agreement applies: SSA's delivery obligation runs from receipt of complete and accurate data.**

---

## D. Substantiation

**D.1 Structure.** The QSEHRA's substantiation program requires, consistent with applicable IRS guidance and the QSEHRA's terms:
**(a)** proof or attestation that the employee and each covered individual has minimum essential coverage, provided **once per year**, at or before the first reimbursement of the year; and
**(b)** an attestation accompanying or with respect to **each request for reimbursement** that the covered individuals continue to have minimum essential coverage for the period to which the request relates.

**D.2 Attestation.** Substantiation under D.1 may be satisfied by the employee's written attestation in the form SSA administers, unless the QSEHRA's terms require documentation from a third party. **SSA may rely on an employee's attestation unless SSA has actual knowledge that it is false.**

**D.3 Effect of non-substantiation.** Where substantiation for a period is not provided, **reimbursement for that period is held** and is released if and when substantiation is provided, subject to the QSEHRA's claim submission deadlines.

**D.4 Contradictory information.** Where SSA receives information indicating that an employee's coverage has terminated or lapsed for a period, **SSA will hold reimbursement for the affected period and refer the matter to Employer**, notwithstanding any attestation covering that period.

**D.5 Records.** Attestation records are retained as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement, identify the version of the attestation text to which the employee responded, and are not modified after capture.

---

## E. No salary reduction; no opt-out

**E.1 Employer funding only.** ⚠️ **A QSEHRA is funded solely by Employer. No salary reduction contribution is permitted, and no cafeteria plan rail is available with respect to the QSEHRA.** SSA's documents prepared under this Rider will not provide for salary reduction to fund the QSEHRA or to pay any portion of an individual health insurance premium in coordination with it.

**E.2 No opt-out mechanism.** ⚠️ **SSA does not administer an opt-out or waiver election with respect to a QSEHRA**, and this Rider provides none. Employer determines the consequences for an eligible employee who does not wish to receive the permitted benefit, in consultation with its own counsel.

**E.3 Excepted benefit premiums.** Premiums for accident-only, specified disease, and other excepted benefit insurance may be paid by salary reduction under a separate cafeteria plan **on their own footing**, where Employer establishes one and where doing so is consistent with Employer's eligible employer status under Section B.1. **Employer determines whether establishing or maintaining any such arrangement affects that status.**

---

## F. Premium tax credit coordination

**F.1 Effect on the credit.** Employer acknowledges that a QSEHRA permitted benefit affects an employee's eligibility for, and the amount of, the premium tax credit, and that an employee applying for advance payment of the credit is required to report the permitted benefit.

**F.2 SSA makes no determination.** ⚠️ **SSA does not determine, compute, or represent whether any QSEHRA offer is affordable for premium tax credit purposes, and furnishes no such figure to any employee.** The notice under A.2 states the permitted benefit amount and the reporting requirement, and nothing further. Section 5.9 of the Agreement applies.

**F.3 No advice to employees.** SSA does not advise any employee whether to claim the credit, whether to obtain coverage, or which coverage to obtain.

---

## G. No shopping, steering, or producer activity

**Section F of RIDER-ICHRA applies to the QSEHRA in full**, as though set out here, including that SSA is not a licensed producer, receives no compensation from any issuer, makes no recommendation and establishes no default, originates no enrollment, furnishes reference information only with no warranty, and is not responsible for the conduct of Employer's broker or agent of record.

⚠️ **One difference from an individual coverage HRA:** a QSEHRA may reimburse individual health insurance coverage without regard to whether the coverage was purchased through an Exchange. The off-Exchange limitation in RIDER-ICHRA Section E is a condition of the cafeteria plan rail, not of an HRA, and **no such limitation is imposed by this Rider.** SSA's non-facilitation posture is unchanged: SSA neither assists nor directs any employee toward any purchase channel.

---

## H. Owners and individuals who cannot participate on a tax-favored basis

Employer determines which individuals cannot receive QSEHRA reimbursements on a tax-favored basis, including more-than-2% shareholders of an S corporation, partners, sole proprietors, and certain family members treated as owners. **SSA applies the tax treatment Employer directs and relies on Employer's determination of status.**

---

## I. Continuation coverage

⚠️ **Continuation coverage with respect to the QSEHRA is outside the scope of this Rider and of APX-COBRA unless expressly elected by addendum.** Employer determines whether any continuation coverage requirement applies to the QSEHRA under federal or state law, in consultation with its own counsel. Where Employer determines that a requirement applies and elects continuation services by addendum, APX-COBRA governs, and the premium is determined and certified by Employer as provided in APX-COBRA Section D.6.

---

## J. Card and funding

Where the electronic payment card is elected, **APX-CARD** and **SCH-BANKING** govern. Because a QSEHRA carries no salary reduction sub-account, the card is funded solely from Employer contributions, and APX-CARD Section C.4's warranty applies without qualification. **A missed or unfunded Employer contribution results in no funding for the affected period and never in participant debt.** APX-CARD Sections A.8 and B.10 apply where the card is used to pay insurance premiums.

---

## K. Regulatory environment and termination

Employer acknowledges that guidance governing QSEHRAs remains limited and is subject to change, that SSA's services are provided against guidance as it exists from time to time, and that Section 4.2 of the Agreement permits SSA to revise fees where a change in law, regulation, guidance, or its interpretation materially revises the nature or scope of the services.

On termination, APX-RA Section E applies, and in addition: SSA ceases delivering notices and collecting substantiation; any card is deactivated; **no employee has any vested right in an unused permitted benefit** except as the QSEHRA's terms provide; and substantiation and notice records are delivered as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement.

## Notes to Kevin

### 1. Two questions scoped out rather than answered

**Continuation coverage (Section I).** From the original review this was flagged as unverified: the Cures Act excludes a QSEHRA from the definition of a group health plan for certain purposes, and I do not know with confidence whether COBRA reaches it. Section I therefore **scopes it out entirely** and makes applicability Employer's determination, with an addendum path if Employer concludes a requirement applies. That is the version that cannot be wrong, and it deliberately does **not** carry the ICHRA answer across — your register warns specifically against that kind of transfer between the two regimes.

**ERISA status (B.6).** Same treatment, for the same reason. Assigned to Employer, not asserted.

### 2. Three real differences from the ICHRA rider, all stated on purpose

| | ICHRA | QSEHRA |
|---|---|---|
| Cafeteria plan rail | Available, off-Exchange only, restrictive "covered by" reading | **Not available at all** (E.1) |
| Opt-out and waiver | Administered, once per plan year | **Not administered — no mechanism provided** (E.2) |
| Purchase channel | Off-Exchange only for the §125 rail | **No limitation** — on- or off-Exchange (G) |

E.2 is the one most likely to surprise someone reading across from the ICHRA rider. ⚠️ **Confirm my understanding that an eligible employee cannot decline a QSEHRA** — I believe that is right and it is why the Rider offers no opt-out, but it is a design consequence worth verifying before issuance rather than after.

### 3. Statutory limits and permitted benefit

A.3 and B.3 split the work the way APX-TRANSIT splits the transit limits: **Employer specifies the annual limit, SSA calculates each employee's permitted benefit against it.** The limits are indexed annually, and you do not want a contract in which you are wrong the year you miss an indexing update. No dollar figure appears anywhere in the Rider.

### 4. The sales constraint that isn't in the document

⚠️ Your register records a decision — Option B, interim — under which **QSEHRA is not offered to Texas employers** pending a Texas certificate of authority, enforced in the sales conversation with **no code gate and nothing in the contract.** Nothing in this Rider changes that, and nothing in it should: a contract term is the wrong place for it. But the consequence is worth stating plainly: **RIDER-QSEHRA should not be a default-available document in the AdobeSign library**, because the only control preventing a Texas QSEHRA sale is someone remembering.

### 5. Section G points at RIDER-ICHRA rather than repeating it

Same reasoning as consolidating APX-FSA and APX-HRA: identical operative text in two documents is how a set drifts. If the non-producer clause ever needs strengthening, it gets strengthened once. ⚠️ The consequence is that **RIDER-QSEHRA cannot be issued without RIDER-ICHRA being in the document library** — which is fine for a template set, but the incorporation by reference should be to the *text* of that Section, not to whether the ICHRA rider itself was elected. It is drafted that way ("applies… as though set out here"); worth confirming that reads cleanly to you.
