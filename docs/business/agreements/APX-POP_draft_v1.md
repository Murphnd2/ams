# SECTION 125 PREMIUM ONLY ARRANGEMENT APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-POP**

Employer has established or will establish a cafeteria plan under Internal Revenue Code §125 under which eligible employees may elect to pay their share of the premium for employer-sponsored coverage by salary reduction on a pre-tax basis (a "premium only arrangement" or "POP"), and has elected POP services in Article VII of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement").

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Agreement. Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date these services are elected.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law.

---

## A. Services SSA provides

**A.1 Plan documents.** SSA will prepare the text of initial drafts of the cafeteria plan document, the summary plan description where one is required, participant election form text, and any amendment or summary of material modification directed by Employer, based on information Employer provides. **Drafts are furnished for Employer's review with its own legal counsel; adoption, execution, and distribution are Employer's alone.**

**A.2 Employee communication.** SSA will provide electronic communication and election material templates at no additional charge. Physical materials are available for an additional fee to be agreed.

**A.3 Nondiscrimination testing.** Once per Plan Year, SSA will conduct the following tests with respect to the cafeteria plan, on data Employer furnishes and certifies:

| Test | Code reference |
|---|---|
| Cafeteria plan eligibility test | §125(g)(3) |
| Cafeteria plan contributions and benefits test | §125(c) / Prop. Treas. Reg. §1.125-7 |
| Key employee concentration test | §125(b)(2) |

SSA will notify Employer if, based on SSA's interpretation, any test does not pass. **Results are informational, are not determinations, and correction of a failed test is Employer's responsibility.** Where Employer also maintains a Plan subject to testing under §105(h) or §129, those tests are provided under APX-RA and not under this Appendix. Additional tests are conducted at Employer's written request for an additional fee.

**A.4 Annual maintenance.** SSA will notify Employer of changes in compliance requirements applicable to cafeteria plans and will prepare document amendments Employer directs. **Such information reflects SSA's interpretation as a benefits administrator and is not tax or legal advice.**

---

## B. Employer responsibilities

**B.1 Adoption and maintenance.** Employer has the exclusive right and duty to adopt, execute, amend, and terminate the cafeteria plan, and to determine which benefits are offered under it.

**B.2 Qualified benefits.** **Employer determines which benefits are qualified benefits eligible for salary reduction under §125.** SSA relies on that determination under Section 5.2 of the Agreement.

**B.3 Eligibility and elections.** Employer establishes eligibility criteria, determines which employees meet them, and collects, retains, and administers participant salary reduction elections and any mid-year election changes. **SSA does not collect, hold, or process participant elections under this Appendix.**

**B.4 Payroll.** Employer, or its payroll provider, implements salary reductions, applies the correct employment tax treatment, and performs all Form W-2 and payroll tax reporting. **SSA does not process payroll and does not verify withholding.**

**B.5 Distribution.** Employer will ensure the plan document is properly executed and will distribute the summary plan description and election materials to eligible employees as applicable law requires.

**B.6 Testing data.** Employer will furnish complete and accurate census, compensation, ownership, and election data on the form SSA provides, and certifies its accuracy.

**B.7 Other obligations.** Employer is responsible for the obligations allocated to it under Section 2.3 of the Agreement with respect to the cafeteria plan and the underlying coverage, and for COBRA with respect to the underlying coverage.

---

## C. Services not included

⚠️ **This Appendix includes documentation and nondiscrimination testing only.** SSA provides none of the following under this Appendix: claims processing or adjudication; account recordkeeping; substantiation; benefit payment, check issuance, or direct deposit; an electronic payment card; election collection or processing; payroll processing; Form 5500 preparation or data; Summary of Benefits and Coverage; or COBRA administration. Each is available only under a separately elected Appendix.

---

## D. Limitation on individual health insurance premiums

⚠️ **This Appendix does not authorize, and SSA's documents prepared under it will not provide for, salary reduction for the premium for individual health insurance coverage constituting major medical coverage**, except where Employer has elected an individual coverage HRA and **RIDER-ICHRA** applies, in which case that Rider governs the availability, scope, and conditions of such salary reduction.

Employer acknowledges that an arrangement under which an employer pays or reimburses the premium for individual major medical coverage may be treated as a group health plan subject to the market reform requirements, and that Employer is responsible for determining the treatment of any benefit it offers under its cafeteria plan.

**Premiums for accident-only, specified disease, and other excepted benefit insurance are addressed on their own footing**, independent of this Section, and Employer determines whether a particular policy qualifies.

---

## E. Termination

On termination, SSA ceases document maintenance and testing as of the effective date, delivers records as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement, and has no further obligation with respect to the cafeteria plan. Employer remains responsible for its obligations as plan sponsor and administrator.

## Notes to Kevin

1. **Why this exists.** A POP client today executes the Tax-Favored appendix, which commits SSA to examining claims, computing amounts payable, disbursing benefits, handling run-off, providing an SBC, and maintaining a funding method — none of which a POP client buys. Section C states the exclusions affirmatively rather than leaving them to inference.
2. **B.3 is the clause to check against practice.** I drafted it so Employer collects and administers elections, because a documentation-and-testing engagement implies no election platform. ⚠️ **If SSA in fact provides an election portal to POP clients, B.3 is wrong** and needs to move into Section A.
3. **Section D is the guardrail I would not omit.** A POP is exactly the vehicle through which an employer stumbles into an employer payment plan — salary-reducing individual major medical premiums with no ICHRA in place. Routing that permission exclusively through RIDER-ICHRA means the capability cannot arrive by accident, and it does so without this Appendix having to take a position on the underlying question.
4. **Testing cites** are the same three as APX-RA's table. ⚠️ Same flag: confirm before issuance, particularly the contributions-and-benefits test, which lives in proposed regulations rather than a clean statutory subsection.
5. **No BAA needed for a POP-only client** — documentation and testing involve no PHI. My recommendation from the BAA notes stands anyway: include it in the envelope. The cost is zero and the scoping argument is one you'd rather not have later.
