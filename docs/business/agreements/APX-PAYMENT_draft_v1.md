# PAYMENT SERVICES APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-PAYMENT**

Employer has elected to have SSA provide payment services in connection with one or more benefit plans administered by SSA under the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement"). This Appendix describes the rights and responsibilities of SSA and Employer with respect to those services.

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Agreement. Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date these services are elected. The responsibilities set forth here are in addition to those set forth in the Agreement.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the payment services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law. The Business Associate Agreement controls as to the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information, subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability, which applies to it.

**Election.** Payment services apply only to the extent elected by Employer in Article V of the Agreement and designated on **SCH-BANKING**. Settlement of electronic payment card transactions is governed by **APX-CARD** and not by this Appendix.

---

## A. Scope of payment services

1. **Check issuance.** SSA prints, signs with Employer's authorized facsimile signature, and releases checks drawn on the Employer-owned account designated on SCH-BANKING, in payment of benefits SSA has processed under the applicable plan.

2. **Participant direct deposit (ACH credits).** SSA originates ACH credit entries to participant accounts in payment of benefits SSA has processed, and originates a corresponding ACH debit entry against the Employer-owned account designated on SCH-BANKING to fund those credits.

3. **Excluded.** SSA does not originate payments except as described above and as instructed by Employer or as produced by SSA's processing of a benefit under the applicable plan. `[Decision: does premium remittance to a carrier or issuer fall under this Appendix, under APX-CARD, or under a separate rail? See notes.]`

---

## B. Account ownership and SSA's authority

1. **The designated account is owned and titled solely by Employer.** SSA has no ownership interest in it, no title to any funds in it, and no right to use funds in it for any purpose other than as authorized under this Appendix and SCH-BANKING.

2. **SSA is not a signatory on, and has no independent access to, any Employer account.** SSA's authority is limited to (a) originating ACH entries Employer has authorized on SCH-BANKING, and (b) issuing checks bearing Employer's own authorized facsimile signature as provided in Section E.

3. **SSA does not hold, receive, or take custody of Employer funds or plan funds** in connection with the services described in this Appendix.

4. Employer may revoke the authorizations on SCH-BANKING at any time by written notice to SSA, effective when SSA and the depository have had a reasonable opportunity to act on it. Revocation does not affect entries already originated or checks already released, and does not relieve Employer of any obligation under Section D.

---

## C. Employer responsibilities

1. **Designation and accuracy.** Employer designates the account on SCH-BANKING and warrants that the financial institution name, routing number, account number, account type, and titling are complete and accurate, and that the account is a business account owned by Employer. Employer will notify SSA in writing of any change **before** it takes effect.

2. **Sufficient funds.** Employer will maintain sufficient available funds in the designated account to cover all entries originated under this Appendix and all checks released under it.

3. **Participant payment data.** Employer is responsible for the accuracy of participant banking information it furnishes, and for the accuracy of any such information furnished by a participant through a portal Employer directs SSA to make available.

4. **Authorization of the facsimile signature.** Where check issuance is elected, Employer furnishes the authorized signature specimen, warrants that the individual is authorized to sign on the account, and authorizes SSA to reproduce it as an electronic image for the sole purpose described in Section E.

5. **Review.** Employer will review payment registers and reports and notify SSA of any error within `[30]` days of their availability.

## D. Funding of participant direct deposit — reimbursement

**This is the section that reflects what actually happens. Read it first.**

1. **Timing.** ACH credit entries to participants and the corresponding ACH debit entry against the designated account are originated in the same processing cycle. **The credits may therefore settle before the funding debit has been finally collected.**

2. **No obligation to advance.** SSA has no obligation to advance its own funds, and nothing in this Appendix or in any prior course of dealing creates one. SSA may at any time, in its sole discretion and without notice, require that funding be finally collected before participant credits are released.

3. **Reimbursement on demand.** If a funding debit against the designated account is returned, rejected, reversed, or otherwise not finally collected for any reason not attributable to SSA's breach of the Standard of Care, **Employer will reimburse SSA the full amount of the participant credits settled against that debit within `[three (3)] business days` of SSA's invoice or written demand.** This obligation is unconditional and is not contingent on Employer's recovery from any participant, depository, or other party.

4. **Returned-entry fee.** A fee of `[$50.00]` is assessed to Employer for each returned or failed ACH entry not attributable to SSA. ⚠️ Confirm against the Fee Schedule; the current authorization form states $50 for the payment rail while the card program provider assesses $25 on card settlement returns.

5. **Interest.** Amounts unreimbursed after the period in Section D.3 bear interest at `[1.5%]` per month or the maximum rate permitted by Michigan law, whichever is less, together with costs of collection including reasonable attorneys' fees.

6. **Set-off.** SSA may set off any amount due under this Section against any funds, credits, fees, or amounts otherwise held by or payable by SSA to Employer, and may apply any Employer reimbursement first to fees and interest and then to principal.

7. **Suspension.** Without terminating this Appendix or the Agreement, SSA may immediately suspend participant direct deposit, check issuance, benefit release, and card funding upon a returned funding entry or a failure to reimburse, and may condition resumption on final collection of funds in advance. **SSA is not liable to Employer, to any plan, or to any participant for any consequence of a suspension made in accordance with this Section.** Employer remains responsible for payment of benefits due under its plans during any suspension.

8. **Repeat failures.** Employer acknowledges that repeated returned entries may result in permanent elimination of the direct deposit payment option for its participants, in SSA's sole discretion.

9. **Reversals are not the remedy.** ⚠️ Employer acknowledges that an ACH credit already settled to a participant generally cannot be recovered by reversal, and that the remedy for a failed funding debit is reimbursement under this Section rather than reversal of the participant credit. SSA will attempt a reversal only where permitted by applicable ACH rules and only for an entry that was erroneous as originated.

10. **Optional — guaranty.** `[Include for closely held employers at SSA's election:]` Employer's obligations under this Section may be supported by a personal guaranty executed on the form SSA provides.

## E. Check issuance

1. SSA prints and releases checks drawn on the designated account, bearing **Employer's own authorized facsimile signature**. SSA does not sign Employer checks in its own name and no SSA personnel is an authorized signatory.

2. SSA stores the signature specimen with the same safeguards it applies to Employer's confidential information, uses it solely to issue checks in payment of benefits SSA has processed, and destroys or returns it on termination or on Employer's written revocation.

3. **Positive pay.** SSA recommends that Employer establish positive pay or an equivalent control with its depository. Employer's decision not to do so is Employer's alone, and SSA is not liable for items its depository pays in the absence of such a control.

4. **Stop payments.** Only Employer may place a stop payment with its depository. SSA will void an unissued or undelivered check on Employer's written request and will provide the information Employer needs to place a stop payment.

5. **Uncashed and stale-dated checks.** Employer is responsible for the disposition of uncashed, stale-dated, and escheatable items under applicable unclaimed-property law. SSA will report outstanding items to Employer `[quarterly]` and will void and reissue an item on Employer's written instruction.

---

## F. Errors and misdirected payments

1. SSA will investigate and work to resolve payment errors asserted by Employer or a participant within `[ten (10)] business days` of receipt of notice.

2. SSA is not responsible for errors, delays, or misdirected payments resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or untimely data furnished by Employer, Employer's payroll provider, or a participant, or from the acts or omissions of any depository, financial institution, or payment network.

3. Where a payment is credited to an incorrect account because of data furnished by Employer or a participant, SSA will make reasonable efforts to recover it. SSA is not liable for amounts not recovered, and Employer remains responsible for the benefit payment due.

4. **Overpayments and ineligible amounts.** SSA will make reasonable efforts to recover an overpayment or a payment of an ineligible amount, including by offset against future benefit payments to the same participant where the plan permits. Consistent with the Agreement, SSA is not liable for amounts not recovered except to the extent the payment resulted directly and proximately from SSA's breach of the Standard of Care.

---

## G. General

1. **Reliance.** SSA may rely on the data, instructions, elections, certifications, and authorizations described in the Agreement's Reliance provision without independent investigation or verification.

2. **Indemnity.** In addition to the indemnities in the Agreement, Employer will indemnify and hold SSA harmless from and against claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses arising out of (a) inaccurate or incomplete account or participant payment data furnished by Employer or a participant, (b) checks issued in accordance with Employer's instructions or bearing Employer's authorized facsimile signature, (c) Employer's failure to fund, and (d) Employer's revocation of an authorization, in each case except to the extent directly and proximately caused by SSA's breach of the Standard of Care. This indemnity is subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability and indemnity procedure.

3. **No fiduciary status.** Nothing in this Appendix confers on SSA any discretionary authority or control over plan assets or plan administration, or makes SSA a fiduciary under ERISA §3(21) or a plan administrator under ERISA §3(16).

4. **Termination.** On termination of this Appendix or the Agreement: SSA ceases originating entries and releasing checks; Employer remains obligated under Section D for amounts already settled; the authorizations on SCH-BANKING terminate once SSA and the depository have had a reasonable opportunity to act; SSA returns or destroys the signature specimen; and outstanding items and unprocessed payment requests are handled as provided in the Agreement.

## Notes to Kevin — decisions and flags

1. **Open decision, carried from the prior turn:** whether PremiumPath **premium remittance to a carrier or issuer** runs through this Appendix, through APX-CARD, or on its own rail. It matters because Section D's reimbursement obligation should reach whatever rail carries it, and because a returned premium draft can lapse a policy — a materially worse failure than a delayed participant reimbursement.
2. **Two numbers to set:** the reimbursement window in D.3 (drafted at 3 business days) and the returned-entry fee in D.4 (drafted at $50 from your current form).
3. **What changed from your existing form.** Added: the reimbursement obligation itself, interest, set-off, suspension, positive pay, stop payments, stale-dated items, misdirected credits, and termination. Removed: the SSA signatory option and the broad "reverse ACH transactions for any errors" authority. Normalized: one defined term throughout ("SSA"), rather than alternating between the DBA and the corporate name as the current form does.
4. **Deliberately absent:** any representation about whether funds in the designated account are ERISA plan assets. The card program provider's own agreement asks the employer to represent they are not; you gain nothing by warranting the answer, and salary-reduction dollars make it a live question rather than a formality.
5. **The absorb-and-chase case you've never hit** is what D.3, D.5, D.6, and D.7 exist for. The design intent is that invoice-and-collect stays the normal path and absorbing becomes a choice you make rather than a position you're left in.
