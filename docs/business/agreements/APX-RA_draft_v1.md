# REIMBURSEMENT ACCOUNT ADMINISTRATION APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-RA**

Employer has established one or more of the following, as elected in Article VII of the Master Services Agreement: a **Health Flexible Spending Arrangement** under Internal Revenue Code §105 offered through a cafeteria plan under §125 ("Health FSA"); a **Dependent Care Assistance Program** under §129 ("DCAP"); a **Health Reimbursement Arrangement** under §105 ("HRA"), which may be a traditional HRA, an **Excepted Benefit HRA** ("EBHRA"), or a **Medical Expense Reimbursement Plan** ("MERP"); an **Individual Coverage HRA** ("ICHRA"); or a **Qualified Small Employer HRA** ("QSEHRA"). Each is a "Plan" and collectively they are the "Plans."

Employer has asked SSA to assist with its administrative obligations under the Plans. This Appendix describes the rights and responsibilities of SSA and Employer with respect to those services.

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement"). Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date these services are elected. The responsibilities set forth here are in addition to those set forth in the Agreement.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law. The Business Associate Agreement controls as to the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information, subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability, which applies to it.

**Riders.** Where an ICHRA is elected, **RIDER-ICHRA** applies in addition to this Appendix and controls over it as to any ICHRA-specific matter. Where a QSEHRA is elected, **RIDER-QSEHRA** applies on the same basis. Sections A through F of this Appendix apply to an ICHRA and a QSEHRA except as a Rider provides otherwise.

**Scope.** Services apply only to the Plans elected in Article VII. SSA provides current and updated information to Employer relating to compliance under Code §§105, 125, 129, and 132, including changes in compliance requirements and notification language. **Such information reflects SSA's interpretation as a benefits administrator and is not tax or legal advice.**

## A. Services SSA provides

**A.1 Plan design assistance.** SSA will assist Employer in evaluating the benefits, terms, and conditions of the Plans and in selecting among available benefit and funding options. **SSA is under no obligation to specify benefits or funding options**, and the selection is Employer's.

**A.2 Plan documents.** SSA will prepare the text of initial drafts of the required plan document or documents, summary plan descriptions, and any summaries of material modification as directed by Employer, based on information Employer provides. **Drafts are furnished for Employer's review with its own legal counsel, and the decision to adopt, execute, and distribute is Employer's alone.**

**A.3 Employee communication and enrollment.** SSA will provide electronic communication and enrollment materials at no additional charge. Physical enrollment kits are available for an additional fee to be agreed.

**A.4 Recordkeeping.** SSA will maintain a record of each Plan participant and separate notational bookkeeping records and accounts based on participants' reimbursement account elections for each Plan Year, including level of coverage, reimbursements, and account balances.

**A.5 Election changes.** SSA will process mid-year election changes in accordance with the terms of the Plans and applicable law. **Elections and changes are prospective only** except where the Plan's terms and applicable law provide otherwise.

**A.6 Claims processing.** SSA will examine each claim for benefits in accordance with the claim review procedures of the Plan and applicable law, take reasonable steps to verify validity, compute the amount payable, and either disburse the benefit due to the extent Employer has made funds available under Section C, communicate amounts due to Employer for payment, or deny the claim in accordance with the Plan and applicable rules and regulations. When disbursing a benefit payment, SSA will provide the participant with an explanation of payment or denial in accordance with the Plan's claim review procedures and applicable regulations, together with an explanation of year-to-date activity in the participant's account.

**A.7 Character of SSA's claim determinations.** ⚠️ **SSA's examination and initial determination of a claim is a ministerial application of Employer's written Plan terms, the Plan's substantiation rules, and applicable law. It is not an exercise of discretion.** SSA may process the initial determination and, where Employer directs, the first level of appeal. **Employer has final authority as to the denial or payment of a claim on appeal and is the claim fiduciary responsible for the final appeal level established under the Plan.** Nothing in this Section makes SSA a fiduciary under ERISA §3(21) or the plan administrator under ERISA §3(16); Section 1.5 of the Agreement governs.

**A.8 Substantiation.** SSA will administer the Plans' substantiation requirements, including requesting documentation of an expense, and will operate any electronic payment card program in accordance with applicable IRS guidance on the substantiation of card transactions. Where a card transaction or a payment is not substantiated or is determined to be for an ineligible expense, SSA will make reasonable efforts to obtain repayment from the participant or to offset the amount against future claims where the Plan permits. **Consistent with the Agreement, SSA is not liable to Employer for an ineligible amount not repaid or not offset, except to the extent it resulted directly and proximately from SSA's breach of the Standard of Care.**

**A.9 Reporting.** SSA will make available to Employer by electronic medium, unless otherwise agreed, a master report showing the payment history and status of participant claims and the amounts and transactions of participant accounts during the Plan Year.

**A.10 Nondiscrimination testing.** Once per Plan Year, SSA will conduct the following tests with respect to the Plans it administers, to the extent applicable to the Plans elected:

| Test | Code reference |
|---|---|
| Cafeteria plan eligibility test | §125(g)(3) |
| Cafeteria plan contributions and benefits test | §125(c) / Prop. Treas. Reg. §1.125-7 |
| Key employee concentration test | §125(b)(2) |
| Self-insured medical reimbursement plan eligibility test | §105(h)(3) |
| Self-insured medical reimbursement plan benefits test | §105(h)(4) |
| Dependent care eligibility test | §129(d)(2)–(3) |
| Dependent care 55% average benefits test | §129(d)(8) |
| Dependent care more-than-5% owner concentration limit | §129(d)(4) |

⚠️ **Cites to be confirmed before issuance — see notes.**

SSA will furnish Employer a form requesting the data necessary to complete the tests, will complete them, and will notify Employer if, based on SSA's interpretation, any test does not pass. **Results are based on information received from Employer and information SSA maintains in the course of its services, are informational, and are not determinations. Correction of a failed test, including any change in elections, benefits, or contributions, is Employer's responsibility.** Additional tests are conducted only at Employer's written request and for an additional fee to be agreed.

**A.11 Form 5500 data.** At Employer's written request, SSA will provide information maintained in its database that is required to be included on Form 5500 with respect to a Plan.

**A.12 Renewal and run-out.** SSA will administer Plan Year renewal, and will administer the Plan's run-out period for claims incurred before, and submitted after, the end of a Plan Year, in accordance with the Plan's terms.

## B. Employer responsibilities

Employer is responsible for all Plan administration not expressly assumed by SSA above, including:

**B.1 Adoption and maintenance.** Employer has the exclusive right and duty to select, implement, amend, or modify the benefits and funding options adopted in connection with the Plans, and to execute the Plan document.

**B.2 Eligibility.** Employer establishes eligibility criteria and determines which employees and dependents have met them.

**B.3 Appeals.** Employer establishes the claims appeal procedure and holds final authority as provided in Section A.7.

**B.4 Prior administration.** SSA has no responsibility for reimbursement requests submitted to, or administration performed by, a prior administrator or by Employer before the effective date of this Appendix, including run-off claims submitted after that date. Employer is responsible for processing them and for maintaining legally required records of them, and will indemnify SSA for any liability relating to them as provided in the Agreement.

**B.5 Information to SSA.** Employer will provide: enrollment forms or information for all new participants and a list of participants terminated or removed from a Plan with their dates of termination; **a confirmation of payroll deductions or contributions on a per-pay-period basis**; and all other information relating to the Plans and their participants necessary for SSA to perform.

**B.6 Plan documents and SPDs.** Employer will ensure that draft documentation SSA provides complies with applicable law in light of Employer's facts, circumstances, and plan designs; will ensure the Plan document is properly executed; and will provide the summary plan description to all participants in accordance with applicable law.

**B.7 Funding.** As provided in Section C.

**B.8 COBRA.** Employer is responsible for its obligations under COBRA with respect to the Plans. SSA provides no assistance except under APX-COBRA.

**B.9 Reporting and disclosure.** Employer is responsible for the obligations allocated to it under Section 2.3 of the Agreement, including Form 5500, Form 720 (PCORI), Code §6055 and §6056 reporting, Form W-2 reporting, and Summary of Benefits and Coverage. **Employer determines which obligations apply to each Plan.** SSA provides no assistance except as expressly assumed here or in a separate appendix for an additional fee.

**B.10 Review.** Employer will review reports SSA posts and notify SSA of any error of which it is aware within `[thirty (30)]` days.

## C. Funding of benefits

**C.1 Employer funds benefits.** Employer will make sufficient funds available to pay all eligible claims presented to SSA. **SSA is under no obligation to advance funds on behalf of Employer**, except to the extent APX-PAYMENT expressly provides for the settlement of participant payments in advance of collection, in which case Employer's reimbursement obligation under that Appendix applies.

**C.2 Methods.** Benefits are funded by one or more of the following, as elected:
**(a) Claims paid directly by Employer.** Employer pays eligible participants based on a payment register SSA submits. SSA will provide reports timely enough to allow Employer to process payment.
**(b) Payment services.** SSA issues checks drawn on, or originates payments funded from, an Employer-owned account, as provided in **APX-PAYMENT** and **SCH-BANKING**.
**(c) Electronic payment card.** Participants pay eligible expenses directly by card, settled as provided in **APX-CARD** and **SCH-BANKING**.

**C.3 Insufficient funds.** If at any time the reimbursement benefits payable exceed the funds Employer has made available, Employer will make the necessary funds available before any further benefit payment is made. SSA's suspension rights under Section 4.5 of the Agreement and under APX-PAYMENT apply.

**C.4 Uniform coverage.** Employer acknowledges that a Health FSA is subject to the uniform coverage rule, under which the full annual election must be available to a participant throughout the Plan Year regardless of contributions made to date, and that **Employer bears the risk of claims paid to a participant whose employment terminates before contributions equal reimbursements.**

**C.5 Experience gains and forfeitures.** Employer determines the disposition of forfeitures and experience gains in accordance with the Plan's terms and applicable law. SSA makes no such determination and takes no interest in any forfeited or unused amount.

## D. Provisions specific to Plan types

**D.1 Health FSA.** Subject to the uniform coverage rule (Section C.4) and to §105(h) testing. Employer elects **either** a carryover **or** a grace period, not both, and elects the run-out period; SSA administers what Employer elects. Employer determines whether the Health FSA is an excepted benefit.

**D.2 DCAP.** Reimbursement is limited to amounts actually credited to the participant's account — **the uniform coverage rule does not apply.** Employer is responsible for the statutory annual limit, for Form W-2 reporting of dependent care benefits, and for §129 testing data. A DCAP is not an ERISA welfare benefit plan; SSA prepares plan document and participant communication text but no summary plan description is required by ERISA.

**D.3 HRA and MERP.** Funded solely by Employer contributions; **no salary reduction contributions are permitted.** Carryforward of unused amounts is as the Plan's terms provide. Subject to §105(h) testing, and — as Employer determines — to COBRA, SBC, PCORI, and §6055 reporting.

**D.4 EBHRA.** Employer determines whether the arrangement satisfies the conditions for excepted benefit status, including the requirement that Employer offer other group health plan coverage, the applicable annual limit, the limits on reimbursement of premiums, and uniform availability. **SSA administers the arrangement as Employer designs it and makes no determination as to excepted benefit status.**

**D.5 ICHRA and QSEHRA.** See RIDER-ICHRA and RIDER-QSEHRA.

## E. Termination

On termination of this Appendix or the Agreement: requests for reimbursement submitted to SSA after the effective date of termination are returned to Employer or, at Employer's request, submitted to another administrator, and SSA has no further responsibility for them; any electronic payment card is deactivated; Employer determines the disposition of remaining account balances and forfeitures under Section C.5; and records are delivered as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement. Employer remains responsible for its obligations as plan administrator.

## Notes to Kevin

### 1. Why this is one appendix and not two

Last turn I said I'd draft APX-FSA and APX-HRA as a pair. Writing them, every section except Section D came out word-for-word identical — claims, substantiation, testing, funding, reporting, run-out, termination. Two documents with identical operative text is how a set drifts: one gets a fix and the other doesn't. So this is **one appendix with a plan-type section**, and the ICHRA and QSEHRA riders sit on top of it rather than duplicating it.

**It changes nothing about how you sell or price.** Article VII still elects Health FSA, DCAP, HRA, EBHRA, and MERP as separate line items, and the Fee Schedule prices them separately. **Splitting it into two later is mechanical** — copy the file, cut Section D. Say the word.

### 2. A.7 is the clause that reconciles the MSA

The Agreement says SSA is never the plan administrator and exercises no discretion, while this Appendix gives SSA initial claim determinations and a first-level appeal. A.7 reconciles them: ministerial application of Employer's written terms, Employer as claim fiduciary on final appeal. ⚠️ **This is the characterization your state TPA licensing analysis rests on, and it is the one a regulator would test.** It is defensible and it is how the industry drafts, but it is a characterization rather than a fact, and the drafting should not be stronger than the practice. If SSA in fact resolves ambiguous claims by judgment rather than by rule, A.7 overstates.

### 3. A.10 — the testing table

Your current appendix lists five tests, mislabels two, cites "§105(h)(2)(A)" for what is actually the eligibility and benefits tests at (h)(3) and (h)(4), omits the §125 contributions-and-benefits test, and omits §129 eligibility. The table fixes all of that.

⚠️ **Confirm the cites before this goes to a client.** A template cite gets quoted back at you for years. The two I would check first: the contributions-and-benefits test, which lives in the proposed §125 regulations rather than in a clean statutory subsection, and §129(d)(4) versus §129(d)(8), which are easy to transpose.

Also new and worth noting: results are **informational**, and **correction is Employer's**. Your current appendix says you will "notify the Employer if any of the tests fail to pass" and stops — which leaves the correction obligation unstated.

### 4. What moved out

- **§132 transit** is gone from the recital. It was never a §125 qualified benefit and does not carry §125, §105(h), or §129 testing, an SPD, a 5500, an SBC, or COBRA. It goes to APX-TRANSIT, deliberately thin.
- **§125 premium-only** goes to APX-POP: document text and §125 testing only, with none of the claims, substantiation, or funding language a POP client currently signs.
- **The retired settlement-account funding language** is gone from Section C entirely. C.2 now names the three real methods and points at APX-PAYMENT and APX-CARD.
- **The SBC clause** is gone. It asserted that HRAs, MERPs, **and excepted FSAs** must furnish an SBC, with a stale 2012 date. Excepted benefits are generally excluded from the SBC requirement. B.9 now allocates the obligation to Employer without adjudicating applicability, which is the version that can't be wrong.

### 5. Decisions open

- B.10 and the Section A.12 run-out period reference: set the review window, and confirm whether you want the run-out length stated here or left entirely to the Plan document. Drafted as the latter.
- D.1: confirm you administer carryover **or** grace period as an either/or. Drafted that way because the Code requires it, but confirm it matches your platform configuration.
