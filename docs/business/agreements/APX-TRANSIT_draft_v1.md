# QUALIFIED TRANSPORTATION FRINGE APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-TRANSIT**

Employer has established or will establish a qualified transportation fringe benefit program under Internal Revenue Code §132(f) providing qualified parking, transit pass, and/or commuter highway vehicle benefits (the "Transit Program"), and has elected transit services in Article VII of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement").

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Agreement. Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date these services are elected.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law.

**Character of the Transit Program.** ⚠️ **A qualified transportation fringe program is not a cafeteria plan under §125, is not a group health plan, and is not an employee welfare benefit plan under ERISA.** Accordingly, and except as Employer determines otherwise, the Transit Program is not subject to nondiscrimination testing under §125, §105(h), or §129; to summary plan description or Form 5500 requirements under ERISA; to the Summary of Benefits and Coverage requirement; or to COBRA. SSA provides no service with respect to any of those requirements under this Appendix.

---

## A. Services SSA provides

**A.1 Program documents.** SSA will prepare the text of initial drafts of a written program description, participant election form text, and participant communication materials, based on information Employer provides. **Drafts are furnished for Employer's review with its own legal counsel; adoption and distribution are Employer's alone.**

**A.2 Elections.** SSA will process participant compensation reduction elections and changes in accordance with the Transit Program's terms. **Elections are prospective only** and may be changed on the frequency the Transit Program permits.

**A.3 Recordkeeping.** SSA will maintain a record of each participant and separate notational bookkeeping accounts reflecting amounts elected, amounts posted from payroll, reimbursements, and balances, by benefit type.

**A.4 Claims and substantiation.** SSA will examine each request for reimbursement against the Transit Program's terms and applicable law, take reasonable steps to verify validity, compute the amount payable, and either disburse to the extent Employer has made funds available, communicate amounts due to Employer, or deny the request with an explanation. **SSA's determination is a ministerial application of Employer's written program terms and is not an exercise of discretion. Employer holds final authority.**

**A.5 Reimbursement and card.** Benefits are paid by the methods elected under Section C. Where an electronic payment card is elected, **APX-CARD** and **SCH-BANKING** govern, and the card is restricted to merchant category codes established for transit and parking.

**A.6 Reporting.** SSA will make available to Employer by electronic medium a report of participant account activity.

**A.7 Limits.** SSA will administer the monthly statutory limit applicable to each benefit type as **Employer specifies it for each calendar year.** ⚠️ **SSA does not determine the applicable statutory limit or its annual indexed amount; Employer determines it and notifies SSA.**

---

## B. Employer responsibilities

**B.1 Adoption and design.** Employer has the exclusive right and duty to adopt, amend, and terminate the Transit Program and to determine which benefit types are offered.

**B.2 Eligibility.** Employer establishes eligibility criteria and determines which employees meet them. **Employer determines whether any individual is eligible for a qualified transportation fringe**, including that partners, more-than-2% S corporation shareholders, sole proprietors, and independent contractors are generally not eligible for this benefit on a tax-favored basis.

**B.3 Statutory limits and tax treatment.** Employer determines the applicable monthly limits, notifies SSA of them for each calendar year, and is responsible for the employment tax treatment of any amount in excess of the excludable limit and for all Form W-2 and payroll tax reporting.

**B.4 Payroll.** Employer, or its payroll provider, implements compensation reductions and furnishes SSA a confirmation of amounts actually withheld on a per-pay-period basis.

**B.5 No cash-out.** ⚠️ Employer acknowledges that **an unused qualified transportation fringe balance generally may not be refunded to a participant in cash or converted to any other benefit**, and that Employer determines the disposition of unused balances under the Transit Program's terms and applicable law. **SSA administers the disposition Employer directs and makes no determination.**

**B.6 Funding.** As provided in Section C.

**B.7 Review.** Employer will review reports SSA posts and notify SSA of any error of which it is aware within `[thirty (30)]` days.

---

## C. Funding of benefits

**C.1 Employer funds benefits.** Employer will make sufficient funds available to pay all eligible reimbursements presented to SSA. **SSA is under no obligation to advance funds**, except to the extent APX-PAYMENT expressly provides.

**C.2 Methods.** As elected: claims paid directly by Employer against a payment register; payment services under **APX-PAYMENT** and **SCH-BANKING**; or the electronic payment card under **APX-CARD**.

**C.3 Amounts posted.** A participant's available balance is limited to amounts actually posted from payroll. **There is no uniform coverage requirement for a Transit Program**, and SSA does not make an amount available in advance of posting.

**C.4 Insufficient funds.** SSA's suspension rights under Section 4.5 of the Agreement and under APX-PAYMENT and APX-CARD apply.

---

## D. Termination

On termination, requests for reimbursement received after the effective date are returned to Employer or forwarded to another administrator at Employer's request; any card is deactivated; Employer determines the disposition of remaining balances under Section B.5; and records are delivered as provided in Section 3.5 of the Agreement.

---

## E. General

Sections 5.2 (reliance), Article V (indemnity and limitation of liability), and Section 1.5 (no fiduciary status) of the Agreement apply.

## Notes to Kevin

1. **This is the document with the largest substantive change from what a transit client signs today.** The current Tax-Favored appendix recites a §125 cafeteria plan "and/or a Code Section 132 Plan," calls them collectively "the Plans," and then commits SSA to §125, §105(h), and §129 testing, an SPD, Form 5500 data, and an SBC — **none of which a §132(f) program requires.** The character paragraph above and the omission of those services is the fix.
2. **A.7 and B.3 push the statutory limit onto Employer on purpose.** The monthly exclusion limits are indexed annually. A contract in which SSA "administers the applicable limit" is a contract in which SSA is wrong the year it misses an indexing update. Employer notifies, SSA administers. ⚠️ Confirm this matches practice — if SSA in fact loads the new limits each year without being told, A.7 should say so and carry the corresponding reliance language instead.
3. **B.5 states the no-cash-out rule without stating its scope.** Deliberate: the rule and its narrow exceptions are Employer's determination, and unused-balance disposition is one of the places a template most easily goes wrong.
4. ⚠️ **Bicycle commuting benefits are deliberately absent.** The exclusion for qualified bicycle commuting reimbursements was suspended by the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, and I do not know the current status with enough confidence to draft it. **VERIFY before adding.** If Employer offers it, it should be addressed by addendum rather than by a template clause that may be wrong.
5. **No BAA is required for a transit-only client** — a §132(f) program is not a group health plan and involves no PHI. Same recommendation as POP: include it anyway.
6. **A.4 carries the same ministerial-determination language as APX-RA A.7**, so the characterization is consistent across every appendix in which SSA touches a claim.
