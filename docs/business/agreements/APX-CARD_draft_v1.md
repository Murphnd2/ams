# ELECTRONIC PAYMENT CARD APPENDIX
**Superior State Administrators, Inc. ("SSA")**
Appendix reference: **APX-CARD**

Employer has elected an electronic payment card for use by participants in one or more Plans administered by SSA, for the purpose of facilitating direct payment of eligible expenses under those Plans.

Cards are issued by a bank (the "Issuer") under a program agreement between the Issuer and SSA's card program provider (the "Program Provider"). **SSA is not the Issuer, is not a bank, and extends no credit.** The Issuer and Program Provider are identified on **SCH-BANKING**.

This Appendix is incorporated into and made a part of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement"). Its effective date is the effective date of the Agreement or, if later, the date these services are elected. The responsibilities set forth here are in addition to those set forth in the Agreement.

**Order of Precedence.** In the event of conflict, this Appendix controls as to the description, scope, and performance of the card services it covers. The Agreement controls as to all other matters, including limitation of liability, indemnification, term, termination, and governing law. The Business Associate Agreement controls as to the use and disclosure of Protected Health Information, subject to the Agreement's limitation of liability, which applies to it.

---

## A. Services SSA provides

**A.1 Availability.** SSA will make the card available to Employer and to eligible participants during the term of this Appendix, except as otherwise provided here. Use of the card is subject to this Appendix and to the cardholder agreement delivered with the card.

**A.2 Third parties.** SSA may engage the Program Provider, the Issuer, and other third parties in order to provide the card. SSA will require each such party that receives Protected Health Information to agree to protections no less than those in the Business Associate Agreement.

**A.3 Administration.** SSA will activate and deactivate cards, respond to participant inquiries, and provide notices regarding participant accounts and actions taken with respect to them, in the manner provided in APX-RA.

**A.4 Errors.** SSA will investigate and work to resolve errors in card transactions asserted by a participant within `[ten (10)] business days` of receipt of notice.

**A.5 Lost or stolen cards.** SSA will promptly cancel any card reported to SSA as lost or stolen. **SSA is not responsible for costs or damages associated with a lost or stolen card that is not properly reported by the participant.**

**A.6 Merchant restrictions and substantiation.** SSA will operate the card program in accordance with applicable IRS guidance on the use of payment cards to pay or reimburse eligible expenses, and the card will be restricted to merchant category codes established for the applicable Plan. **A purchase at a merchant within a permitted merchant category code does not establish that the expense is eligible under the Plan.** Substantiation, repayment, and offset are administered as provided in APX-RA Section A.8.

**A.7 Deactivation.** SSA will deactivate a card on termination of the participant's employment, on termination of the participant's coverage under the applicable Plan, or where SSA, the Program Provider, or the Issuer determines that the card has been used in violation of the cardholder agreement or applicable Plan terms.

**A.8 No merchant acceptance warranty.** ⚠️ **SSA does not warrant that the card will be accepted by any particular merchant, carrier, insurer, or payment portal.** Card acceptance depends on the merchant's category code assignment, the merchant's own systems, and the Issuer's and payment network's authorization processing, none of which SSA controls.

**A.9 Employer data.** Neither SSA nor the Program Provider is responsible for errors resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or untimely data provided by Employer or a participant.

---

## B. Settlement and funding

**B.1 Settlement.** Card transactions are settled by ACH debit against the Employer-owned account designated on **SCH-BANKING**, generally on the business day following the transaction. Employer authorizes that debit on SCH-BANKING.

**B.2 Employer is responsible to the Program Provider.** ⚠️ **Employer understands and agrees that it is responsible to the Program Provider for settlement of card transactions.**

**B.3 Returned entries.** ⚠️ **A charge of $25.00 will be assessed to Employer for each ACH entry returned due to insufficient funds.**

**B.4 Program Provider's remedies.** ⚠️ **The Program Provider reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to terminate access to its system by Employer's plan participants if payment obligations are not paid in full in a timely manner.**

**B.5 Third-party beneficiary.** **The Program Provider is an intended third-party beneficiary of Sections B.2 through B.4 and of Part 5 of SCH-BANKING**, notwithstanding Section 6.14 of the Agreement. This Appendix and SCH-BANKING may be produced to the Program Provider on request.

**B.6 Sufficient funds.** Employer will maintain sufficient available funds in the designated account to cover all settlement entries. Employer will notify SSA in writing before any change to the designated account takes effect, and is responsible for losses resulting from account information it has furnished that is inaccurate or out of date.

**B.7 Suspension.** ⚠️ Without terminating this Appendix or the Agreement, **SSA may suspend card funding, and may deactivate cards, upon a returned settlement entry, a failure to maintain sufficient funds, or a failure to pay amounts due.** SSA will use reasonable efforts to notify Employer before suspending and will restore funding promptly on final collection of the amounts due. **SSA is not liable to Employer, to any Plan, or to any participant for any consequence of a suspension made in accordance with this Section.** Employer remains responsible for benefits due under its Plans during any suspension.

**B.8 No advance; no participant debt.** SSA does not advance funds for card settlement. **A missed, late, or unfunded payroll or employer contribution results in no card funding for the affected period, and never in a debt owed by a participant.**

**B.9 Set-off and indemnity.** SSA may set off amounts due under this Appendix as provided in APX-PAYMENT. Employer will indemnify SSA as provided in Section 5.6 of the Agreement for amounts arising out of Employer's failure to fund settlement, Employer's failure to execute or maintain the authorization on SCH-BANKING, and inaccurate account information Employer has furnished.

**B.10 Lapse of insurance coverage.** ⚠️ Where the card is used to pay insurance premiums, Employer acknowledges that a declined, suspended, or unfunded card transaction may result in the lapse of a participant's insurance coverage, and that **SSA does not guarantee that any premium payment will be authorized, funded, or honored.** RIDER-ICHRA Section H.3 applies where an individual coverage HRA is elected.

## C. Funding sources and sub-accounts

**C.1 Separate ledgering.** Where a card is funded from more than one source — including an employer contribution under a health reimbursement arrangement and an amount withheld from a participant's wages under a cafeteria plan — **each funding source is separately ledgered as its own sub-account**, and SSA applies the ordering rule the applicable Plan terms establish.

**C.2 Employer certification.** ⚠️ For each contribution file or funding instruction Employer submits, **Employer certifies that each amount reported is either (a) an employer contribution Employer has authorized under the applicable Plan, or (b) an amount actually withheld from the identified participant's wages for the stated pay period.** SSA relies on that certification under Section 5.2 of the Agreement and does not verify it against payroll records.

**C.3 Funding limited to posted amounts.** A card is funded only to the extent of amounts actually posted from an authorized funding source. **SSA does not fund a card in anticipation of a contribution or withholding not yet posted**, except to the extent the applicable Plan's terms require an amount to be available irrespective of contributions made to date, in which case APX-RA Section C.4 governs and Employer bears the corresponding risk.

**C.4 No employee contributions to certain arrangements.** Where a Plan does not permit salary reduction contributions — including a health reimbursement arrangement other than through a separate cafeteria plan sub-account — **Employer warrants that no amount it reports for that Plan consists of employee contributions.**

## D. Participants and cardholders

**D.1 Eligibility.** Employer is responsible for ensuring that cards are issued only to employees who are active participants in one or more Plans for which the card is elected.

**D.2 No card to a non-employee.** ⚠️ **A card will not be issued to, and will not be retained by, an individual who is not an active employee, including a qualified beneficiary participating under continuation coverage.** APX-COBRA Section E.4 applies.

**D.3 Dependent cards.** Additional cards may be issued to a participant's designated dependents where the Plan and the card program permit.

**D.4 Cardholder agreement.** Each cardholder is subject to the cardholder agreement delivered with the card, which is between the Issuer and the cardholder. **SSA is not a party to it.** As part of enrollment, each participant acknowledges the provisions of the card program described in that agreement.

**D.5 Receipts.** Each cardholder must retain the invoice or receipt for each transaction and provide it to SSA, Employer, or the IRS on request.

**D.6 Ineligible expenses.** Where a card is used for an expense that is not eligible under the Plan, repayment and offset are administered as provided in APX-RA Section A.8. **Failure to make repayment may result in suspension of the cardholder's charging privileges.** Employer acknowledges that immediate repayment of an ineligible amount may be requested from the cardholder to the extent the Plan's terms and applicable law permit, and that Employer should confirm with its own counsel that such action is permitted under its Plan.

**D.7 Notification of terminations.** Employer will notify SSA of the termination of a cardholder's employment or coverage. **Employer will hold SSA harmless from liability for transactions honored after a termination as to which Employer failed to notify SSA.**

## E. Data

**E.1 Residency.** Employer acknowledges that data necessary to provide the card program resides on servers owned by or operated on behalf of SSA or its third-party vendors.

**E.2 Processing rights.** Employer grants SSA, the Program Provider, and the Issuer the right to receive, process, and perform all services required in accordance with the data submitted for the card program.

**E.3 Aggregate data.** SSA may derive and use aggregate and statistical data from such information as provided in Section 6.7 of the Agreement. Aggregate or statistical data derived from Protected Health Information is de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. §164.514.

## F. Termination

On termination of this Appendix or the Agreement: all cards are deactivated as of the effective date of termination or such earlier date as SSA determines necessary to limit settlement exposure; **Employer remains obligated to fund settlement of all transactions authorized before deactivation**, including transactions settling after the effective date of termination; the authorization on SCH-BANKING terminates once SSA, the Program Provider, and the depository have had a reasonable opportunity to act on the termination; and any residual balance is disposed of as the applicable Plan's terms provide.

---

## G. General

**G.1 Reliance.** Section 5.2 of the Agreement applies to all data, certifications, and instructions Employer furnishes under this Appendix, including the certification in Section C.2.

**G.2 Indemnity and liability.** Article V of the Agreement governs, including the limitation of liability.

**G.3 No fiduciary status; no funds held.** Section 1.5 and Section 1.6 of the Agreement govern. **SSA does not hold or take custody of funds in connection with card settlement**, which is effected by ACH debit against Employer's own account.

## Notes to Kevin

### 1. What replaces the retired settlement-account model

Your current card appendix is built entirely on the model you no longer use: A.5 has SSA or the third party "make available a non-interest bearing settlement account into which Employer must deposit the Minimum Balance," and B.3 has Employer depositing a minimum balance that SSA may increase at its discretion, with notice-and-withdraw replenishment. **All of that is gone.** Section B is next-business-day ACH settlement against the employer's own account, which is what actually happens.

### 2. B.2, B.3, and B.4 are the flow-downs you warrant when you click the Summit acknowledgment

They appear here in the operative terms and again on SCH-BANKING Part 5 where the employer signs, with the Program Provider named an intended third-party beneficiary in both. **That is what turns a click-through warranty into a document you can produce.** B.5 carries the carve-out from the MSA's no-third-party-beneficiary clause.

### 3. C.4 replaces the warranty that PremiumPath breaks

Your current B.4 has the employer warrant that all funds "consist of employer contributions only and do not consist of employee contributions." That is flatly incompatible with a cafeteria plan salary reduction rail. **C.1 through C.4 replace it:** separate sub-accounts per funding source, an employer certification as to what each reported amount actually is, funding limited to posted amounts, and the employer-contributions-only warranty **narrowed to the arrangements where it is actually true.**

C.2's certification is deliberately worded as the recordkeeper-reliance standard — *amounts actually withheld from the identified participant's wages for the stated pay period* — so the contract says the same thing your file handoff says. ⚠️ **Confirm that wording matches what the platform actually collects**, because the value of the certification is that the two are identical.

### 4. A.8 and B.10 are the two clauses I'd not remove

**A.8** disclaims any warranty that the card will be accepted at a given merchant or carrier portal. Your own build notes treat live authorization at a real carrier portal as an unresolved gate. Until that is proven, a contract that implies premium payment capability is a promise ahead of the product.

**B.10** says a declined or unfunded transaction can lapse coverage and that SSA does not guarantee authorization or funding. This is the contract-side counterpart to the same risk RIDER-ICHRA H.3 addresses, and it is materially more consequential than the FSA case: a declined drugstore purchase is an inconvenience; a declined premium draft can end a policy.

### 5. B.7 suspension — read this one against PremiumPath

Suspension is the right remedy and it has to be in the document. But suspending card funding for an FSA client delays reimbursements, while suspending it for a PremiumPath client can lapse policies. B.7 therefore has **reasonable efforts to notify before suspending** and **prompt restoration on collection**, which the payment-rail version does not. `[Decision: whether you want a stated notice period — e.g. two business days — rather than "reasonable efforts." A stated period is friendlier to the client and slower for you.]`

### 6. Kept from the current appendix

A.1 through A.7 and A.9 are substantially your existing text, cleaned up: the Rev. Rul. 2003-43 citation is generalized to "applicable IRS guidance" so the clause does not go stale as guidance develops, the dangling cross-reference to a "Reimbursement Account Service Appendix" now resolves to APX-RA, and the recital covers all elected Plans rather than only Health FSA, HRA, and §132. E.1 through E.3 are your existing data clauses, with the aggregate-data right now pointing at the MSA where it belongs.
