## Strategic Positioning

### The Core Message
"AMS is the operational business layer that makes the DataPath ecosystem complete."

### The Market Gap (Research-Validated)
**No competitor builds TPA operational management software.** Every major platform — Summit, Alegeus, WEX — is a claims engine. The space between "process claims" and "run a TPA business" is held together by spreadsheets, generic CRMs (Salesforce, Monday.com, PandaDoc), and manual processes. AMS occupies genuinely uncontested territory.

### Three Solutions Framework
1. **Client Operations Management** — Standardized renewal and ticket workflows. Reusable Sequences. Automated task assignment. New hires productive on Day 1. Addresses staff turnover.
2. **Integrated Sales Engine** — Complete quote-to-implementation pipeline. AI-powered proposal generation. Self-service agency portals. Line-of-service scoping per partner.
3. **Vendor Integration & Delegated Operations** — Multi-tenant architecture for BPO/third-party vendors. "Accelergent is the hands, AMS is the brain." Extends to any vendor a PSP engages.

### ProsperityHSA Alignment (The Sherry Slide)

DataPath is executing the most ambitious strategic pivot in its 40-year history — transforming from a TPA software vendor into an HSA infrastructure company.

**Two bank acquisition channels exist:**
- **Channel 1: DataPath Direct** — DataPath/ProsperityHSA signs banks directly.
- **Channel 2: PSP-Sourced** — A Summit PSP brings their own banking relationship. The onboarding implementation gets outsourced back to DataPath/Accelergent as BPO.

**AMS serves both channels through two layers:**

**Layer 1 — Bank as Customer:**
- AMS manages the bank implementation as a Sequence — white-label platform configuration, deposit account integration, card program setup, contact center training, compliance documentation, go-to-market readiness.
- Note: The 14-week bank implementation is about establishing the banking/technology partnership, NOT about employer-level benefit plan setup (HDHP, Section 125, etc.). That's Layer 2.
- Every milestone tracked across DataPath, Accelergent, and the bank's team.

**Layer 2 — Bank as Agency:**
- Once the bank is live, it functions as an agency within AMS.
- The bank's relationship managers generate branded, bank-customized proposals for their commercial clients through a self-service portal.
- Each employer group flows into standard AMS workflows for FSA, HRA, ICHRA, COBRA alongside HSA.
- One bank partnership creates a self-sufficient sales channel.
- The volume multiplier: one bank = hundreds of employer groups.

### Value to DataPath (Four Pillars)
1. **New Revenue — Today:** AMS is distributable now. License to Summit clients as recurring SaaS. No development required.
2. **ProsperityHSA Accelerator:** Operational infrastructure to onboard bank partners and scale employer activation without proportional headcount.
3. **Client Retention:** When workflows live in the DataPath ecosystem — not just claims — switching costs increase dramatically.
4. **Zero Roadmap Disruption:** AMS is a different category than Summit. No resource conflict with existing development.

### Market Expansion (Platform-Agnostic)
- AMS works beyond Summit — Alegeus and WEX PSPs face the same operational gaps.
- DataPath can license AMS to competitors' clients (revenue from clients they couldn't reach before).
- Every AMS license creates a DataPath relationship with a competitor's client (lower-risk path to Summit).
- AMS positions DataPath as the operational hub for the entire TPA industry.

### HSA Market Landscape — ProsperityHSA Competitive Context

**Three tiers of HSA players:**

**Tier 1 — The Giants (Direct-to-Employer):**
- **HealthEquity:** 9.9M HSAs, 17.1M total accounts, 120K+ organizations. Largest non-bank custodian. Full CDH stack (HSA, FSA, HRA, COBRA, commuter). Acquired WageWorks (2019) and Further (2021). Their bank partnerships typically involve banks EXITING HSA administration — HealthEquity takes over accounts. Opposite of ProsperityHSA's model.
- **Fidelity:** 4.4M HSA holders. Highest Morningstar scores. Zero fees. Leverages massive retirement platform.
- **Optum Bank / UnitedHealth:** Large bank-based custodian tied to UHC ecosystem.
- **HSA Bank (Webster Bank):** Traditional bank-based custodian.

**Tier 2 — Platform Players (White-Label for TPAs):**
- **Alegeus:** White-label CDH including HSA. 300+ TPA clients. Powers TPAs who serve employers.
- **WEX:** Payments-first, HSA through benefits platform. Also white-label.
- **DataPath/Summit:** Traditional TPA-serving platform with HSA.

**Tier 2.5 — ProsperityHSA's Unique Position (White-Label HSA for Banks):**
ProsperityHSA creates a new channel. Traditional model: Employer → TPA → HSA Custodian. ProsperityHSA model: **Bank → DataPath (custodian + administrator + BPO) → Bank's Commercial Clients (Employers) → Employees.** The bank is the distribution channel, not the TPA.

**Why banks care about ProsperityHSA:**
- 68% of HSA assets reside outside traditional banks — deposits they're losing
- 60% of banking customers willing to switch for better financial wellness features
- HSA assets projected to reach $199B by 2027
- Banks get: fee revenue, deposit growth, deeper commercial relationships, stickier consumer banking

**ProsperityHSA vs. HealthEquity bank model:**
HealthEquity's bank partnerships = bank exits HSA, HealthEquity takes accounts. Acquisition play.
ProsperityHSA = bank KEEPS the relationship, keeps deposits (FDIC), keeps brand. DataPath does everything behind the curtain. Fundamentally different pitch: "grow your HSA business" vs. "hand us your HSA business."

**Three-Entity ProsperityHSA Architecture:**
1. DataPath, Inc. — Summit technology platform (administration, portal, mobile app, card processing)
2. DataPath Financial Services (DFS) — IRS-approved non-bank custodian (since Nov 2023). Holds accounts, compliance, investment advisory via DataPath Investment Advisors (DPIA)
3. Accelergent Growth Solutions — BPO, contact center, marketing, fulfillment

**What a bank gets:** White-labeled portal + mobile app, COMPASS debit card in bank branding, FDIC deposits stay at bank, investment options, branded contact center, marketing materials, go-to-market support, compliance handled by DFS. "Minimal IT resources" from bank.

**Where AMS fills gaps in the ProsperityHSA model:**
1. **Bank Partner Pipeline Management** — Tracking bank prospects through sales cycle (AMS sales engine)
2. **Bank Implementation Orchestration** — Multi-party implementation Sequence (DataPath + DFS + Accelergent + bank teams)
3. **Employer Group Activation** — Bank's commercial RMs approach business clients; each employer needs HDHP verification, plan setup, contributions, payroll coordination, enrollment (AMS Setups, Checklists)
4. **Cross-Sell to Full CDH** — Once employer is on HSA, they need FSA, HRA, ICHRA, COBRA, LSA. Bank-as-agency generates proposals for full suite (AMS Proposal Builder)
5. **Annual Renewal Management** — Contribution limit updates, plan amendments, re-enrollment across hundreds of employer groups per bank (AMS Sequences)
6. **Multi-Bank Visibility** — As ProsperityHSA scales to 10-50 bank partners, operational visibility across all implementations and employer groups (AMS dashboard/filtering)

**Why DataPath needs this and HealthEquity doesn't:** HealthEquity is Tier 1 — they work directly with large employers, they ARE the operation with dedicated implementation teams. DataPath's model powers a distributed network of intermediaries (banks, TPAs, brokers) who each need operational tooling. That intermediary layer is exactly where AMS lives.

**Kevin's honest assessment:** HSA/ProsperityHSA is the biggest opportunity but least immediately ready — would need some modifications/improvements to optimize for bank onboarding workflows specifically. However, the core engine (Sequences, Setups, Agency portal, Proposal Builder) maps structurally. Kevin's strength is the ability to visualize solutions and translate them into useful functionality.

### Partnership Models
- **Path A — License & Distribute:** DataPath distributes AMS to Summit clients. Recurring SaaS revenue share. Kevin continues active development. Rapid time-to-market.
- **Path B — Acquisition:** DataPath acquires AMS and full IP. Integrate as native platform component. Kevin available for transition & development. Maximum long-term strategic value.
- Kevin's preference is acquisition, but licensing is the acceptable floor.
- Accelergent integration is a deployment strategy within either model, not a standalone option.

- The bank is both the **prospect** (being sold to) and the **agency** (will sell to its commercial clients)
- Agency role-based access already exists in AMS — agents access the same site as the PSP with scoped permissions

**New Feature Concept — Agent Delegation on Setups:**
- For Setups specifically, allow agents of the sales agency that closed the sale to be delegated tasks/todos
- Filter available agents to only those belonging to the agency tied to that specific setup
- Would require a new view for agent-delegated tasks
- This enables banks (as agencies) to receive and complete checklist-type tasks during their own onboarding
- **Applies beyond ProsperityHSA** — any setup could benefit from delegating tasks to the selling agency's people
- This is a general platform improvement, not just an HSA feature
