# PremiumPath messaging register

Settled 2026-09-10 (session 43). Governs all PremiumPath marketing, proposal HTML, and employee communications for the ICHRA + §125 bundle.

## Audiences

| Surface | Audience | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Agency-branded PremiumPath page | Downstream agents (through SWBD) | Fee margin and persistency copy allowed here only |
| Proposal custom pages | Employer / decision maker, agent-composed and agent-sent (LA-17) | Carrier-neutral; no agent markup; no SSA-to-employer paper |
| Employee communications | Employees | SSA-drafted; delivered under the establishment fee |

## Positioning

"Every employee gets a way to pay, and every policy stays paid." The three paths are described by what the employee buys (off-Marketplace, on-Marketplace, supplemental), never by product. PremiumPath never picks a plan: the agent advises and the employee decides.

## Red-flag register

| Never say | Say instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Free coverage" / "costs employees nothing" | "A stipend and pre-tax payroll options help employees pay for coverage they choose" | Implies employer funding of individual premiums |
| "Your stipend goes on your card" / "premium allowance" | "Additional taxable pay — and, separately, an optional payroll election" | Conditions the stipend |
| Any carrier name | Carrier-neutral categories | Conflicts with SSA's no-endorsement disclaimer and LA-09 |
| "Health insurance" / "alternative to major medical" for excepted benefits | "Supplemental coverage — not major medical, not minimum essential coverage" | Substitute-for-comprehensive framing |
| The ICHRA "designed to be unaffordable" / "so they keep subsidies" | Nothing public. The agent discusses affordability with the employer only | Reads as engineering the subsidy |
| SEP as a headline next to the ICHRA amount | SEP in agent training and FAQ only | Invites the question whether the ICHRA is a real benefit |
| "Pre-tax premiums for everyone" / owners saving tax | "Eligible employees may pay eligible premiums pre-tax"; owners get "the same payroll autopay, post-tax" | §318 attribution excludes most owners |
| "Pays for itself" | "Payroll-tax savings on pre-tax elections can offset the fee" | True per participant, not per census |
| "We help employees pick a plan" | "Your agent advises; we administer" | No-recommendation boundary |
| "Any carrier, any plan" | "Wherever the carrier accepts card payment" | Not every carrier accepts the card |
| "Pre-tax" for supplemental coverage | Card-paid, with no tax claim | Pre-tax supplemental is gated on the carrier's written resolution of its premium-payer clause |
