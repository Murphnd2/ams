Flexible Spending Accounts

The Benefit That Pays for Itself

Most employees are already spending money on healthcare — copays, prescriptions, glasses, dental work, daycare. A Flexible Spending Account lets them pay for those expenses with pre-tax dollars, which means more take-home pay on every paycheck. And because pre-tax contributions reduce your payroll tax obligation as the employer, you save money too.

The math: For every dollar an employee contributes to an FSA, they save roughly 20–30% in combined taxes. On the employer side, you save 7.65% in FICA/Medicare matching on every pre-tax dollar. For a company with 20 employees averaging $2,000 each, that's over $3,000 a year in employer payroll tax savings — at zero additional cost.

An FSA is established under a Section 125 Cafeteria Plan — the IRS framework that makes pre-tax benefits possible. Superior State handles the plan document, the compliance, the claims, and the debit cards — you just offer the benefit and let your employees start saving.

Four Types of FSAs

Each Designed for a Different Need

Healthcare FSA
The most widely used FSA. Pre-tax dollars for qualified medical, dental, and vision expenses — copays, deductibles, prescriptions, eyeglasses, dental cleanings, orthodontia, physical therapy, mental health services, and hundreds of OTC products. Full annual election available day one. No health plan required.
2026 Limit: $3,400Carryover: up to $680
Dependent Care FSA (DCAP)
Helps working parents pay for daycare, preschool, before/after school programs, summer day camps, and elder care. Funds available as contributed (not upfront). Both spouses must have earned income. Eligible dependents: children under 13, or any dependent incapable of self-care.
2026 Limit: $7,500New for 2026
Limited Purpose FSA (LPFSA)
For employees with an HSA. Covers dental and vision expenses only, preserving HSA eligibility. Eye exams, glasses, contacts, dental exams, cleanings, fillings, crowns, braces, LASIK. Essential for any employer offering an HDHP with HSA.
2026 Limit: $3,400Carryover: up to $680
Individual Premium Reimbursement FSA
Pre-tax payment for individually purchased health-related insurance premiums — dental, vision, critical illness, cancer, hospital indemnity, accident, disability. Not major medical. Separate election from Healthcare FSA. Employees can participate in both.
